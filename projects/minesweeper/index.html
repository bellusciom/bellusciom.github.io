Automated Minesweeper Solver

A constraint-propagation solver that plays randomly generated minesweeper fields.

The solver works on the boundary between revealed and hidden territory. Each revealed number is a constraint on its $3 \times 3$ neighborhood, and three rules are applied in order of strength. If a cell's count equals its hidden plus flagged neighbors, every hidden neighbor is a mine (flag them all). If the count is already met by flags, every remaining hidden neighbor is safe (reveal them all). When neither rule fires, a subset rule compares the cell with each neighboring revealed number: intersecting their neighborhoods splits the hidden cells into shared and exclusive regions, and bounding how many mines the shared region can absorb sometimes pins down the exclusive cells exactly, flagging or revealing cells that neither constraint could decide alone. Only when no rule yields a move does the solver fall back on a random guess, the one step that can lose the game.

Run the solver

Generate a random field and watch the solver play, or play it yourself — click to reveal, right-click to flag. "Step" applies one full pass of the rules (all moves the pass finds are played at once, as in the script); "Auto-solve" repeats passes until the board is won or a guess hits a mine. Guesses are counted so you can see how often luck was needed.
